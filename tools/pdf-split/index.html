PDF Split

The PDF Split tool extracts selected pages or page ranges from a PDF and saves them as a new file, right in your browser.

How to use the PDF Split

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Type the pages you want, like 1-3, 5, 8-10.
  3. Press Extract pages.

About the PDF Split

Pull out just the pages you need, such as pages 1 to 3 and 7, from a long PDF. Choose the file, enter the page numbers or ranges and download the new PDF. It runs on your device, so confidential documents are not uploaded. Use it before emailing a document when you only want to share part of it.

Tips

  • Use commas and dashes, for example 1-3, 7, 10-12.
  • Check the page count shown after choosing your file.
  • Merge the result with other files using the PDF Merge tool if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save every page separately?

Yes. Use 'Every page as separate file'.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No.

How do I enter page ranges?

Type pages separated by commas and use a dash for ranges, for example 1-3, 7, 10-12.

Does splitting change the quality?

No. Pages are copied as they are, so quality stays the same.
