Discount Calculator

The Discount Calculator shows the final price after a percentage discount and how much money you save.

How to use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage (and an extra one if any).
  3. See what you pay and what you save.

About the Discount Calculator

Enter the original price and the discount percentage to see the sale price and the saving. You can also add a second discount, such as an extra coupon, to see the combined effect. Remember that two discounts of 20% and 10% do not equal 30%, because the second applies to the already reduced price. The calculation is done locally and nothing is saved.

Tips

  • Compare offers by the final price, not just the percentage.
  • Add shipping and taxes before deciding which deal is cheaper.
  • Remember that stacked discounts apply one after another.

Frequently asked questions

How does a second discount work?

The second discount is applied on the already reduced price, not the original.

Can I use it for sale shopping?

Yes, it is great for quick checks.

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to get the saving, then subtract it from the price.

Is a 20% plus 10% discount the same as 30%?

No. 20% off then 10% off equals 28% off, because the 10% applies to the reduced price.
