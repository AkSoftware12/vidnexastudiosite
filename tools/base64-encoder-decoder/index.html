Base64 Encoder & Decoder

The Base64 Encoder Decoder converts text to Base64 and decodes Base64 text back to the original, with Unicode support.

How to use the Base64 Encoder & Decoder

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Press Encode or Decode.
  3. Copy the result.

About the Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Base64 turns data into plain text characters so it can travel safely in URLs, emails, JSON and HTML. Paste text to encode or a Base64 string to decode and see the result at once. Base64 is not encryption, so do not use it to hide secrets. The tool runs in your browser and handles non-English characters correctly.

Tips

  • Remember that Base64 is encoding, not security.
  • If decoding fails, check for missing characters or extra spaces.
  • Use the Image to Base64 tool when you want to encode a picture.

Frequently asked questions

Does it support Hindi and emoji?

Yes. Text is handled as UTF-8.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. It is only an encoding and anyone can decode it.

Is Base64 secure?

No. Anyone can decode it, so it should never be used to protect passwords or private data.

Why does decoding show strange characters?

The data may not be text, or the string may be damaged. Check that it is copied completely.
