Password Generator

The Password Generator creates strong, random passwords of the length you choose, using letters, numbers and symbols.

How to use the Password Generator

  1. Choose a length.
  2. Pick the character types.
  3. Press New password and copy it.

About the Password Generator

Pick a length, choose which character types to include and copy a new password. It uses your browser's cryptographic random generator, and the password is created on your device and never sent or stored. Longer passwords are stronger than short complex ones, so aim for at least 14 characters. Store passwords in a reliable password manager and use a different one for each account.

Tips

  • Use at least 14 characters for important accounts.
  • Never reuse a password across sites; use a password manager.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe?

Passwords are made on your device with the browser's secure random generator and are never sent anywhere.

How long should a password be?

Use at least 14 characters, and a different password for every site.

Is the generated password stored anywhere?

No. It is created in your browser and disappears when you close the page.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 characters, and 14 or more for email, banking and other important accounts.
