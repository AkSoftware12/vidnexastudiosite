Lorem Ipsum Generator

The Lorem Ipsum Generator creates placeholder paragraphs, sentences or words so you can fill a design or mockup before the real copy is ready.

How to use the Lorem Ipsum Generator

  1. Choose how many paragraphs, sentences or words.
  2. Press Generate.
  3. Copy the text into your design.

About the Lorem Ipsum Generator

Designers and developers use lorem ipsum because it looks like real text without distracting reviewers with meaning. Choose how many paragraphs, sentences or words you need, generate the text and copy it into your layout. It is useful for testing fonts, line lengths, card heights and responsive breakpoints. Generation happens in your browser instantly and there is nothing to install.

Tips

  • Generate a little more text than you need, then trim; it is easier than adding.
  • Test both short and long blocks to see how your layout behaves with different amounts of text.
  • Replace the placeholder with real copy before publishing, because search engines prefer meaningful content.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lorem Ipsum?

It is dummy text used by designers so a layout can be judged without real content.

Is the text random?

Yes. Each press builds new text from a list of Latin-style words.

What does lorem ipsum mean?

It is scrambled Latin-style filler text used since the 1500s by printers and designers as a stand-in for real content.

Is it free to use in commercial projects?

Yes. Lorem ipsum is placeholder text with no copyright restrictions.
