PDF Page Organizer

The PDF Page Organizer shows every page of a PDF as a thumbnail so you can rotate, delete, reorder and add pages, then download the new PDF.

How to use the PDF Page Organizer

  1. Choose a PDF and wait for the page previews.
  2. Use the buttons under each page to move, rotate or delete it.
  3. Add another PDF or a blank page if you need to.
  4. Press Download PDF.

About the PDF Page Organizer

Fix a scanned file with sideways pages, remove a blank page, move the last page to the front or insert pages from a second PDF. Each page has its own buttons to move it left or right, rotate it either way or delete it. You can also add a blank page. The pages are rearranged inside your browser, so documents are not uploaded. Large PDFs take longer to show their thumbnails.

Tips

  • Rotate pages before you move them, so the preview is easy to read.
  • Use Add another PDF to combine files and sort them in one step.
  • Check the page count shown at the top before downloading.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine pages from two PDFs?

Yes. Use Add another PDF and then arrange the pages in the order you want.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The pages are rearranged on your device.

Can I delete several pages?

Yes. Use the delete button on each page you do not want, then download.

Why do thumbnails load slowly?

Each page is drawn one by one on your device. Long PDFs and scanned files with big images take longer.
