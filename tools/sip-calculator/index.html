SIP Calculator

The SIP Calculator estimates the future value of a monthly SIP investment from the monthly amount, expected annual return and number of years.

How to use the SIP Calculator

  1. Enter the monthly amount.
  2. Enter the expected yearly return and years.
  3. See invested amount, returns and total value.

About the SIP Calculator

A systematic investment plan puts a fixed amount into a fund every month. Enter your monthly amount, the return you expect and the period, and the calculator shows the total invested, the estimated gains and the final value. It makes the effect of compounding easy to see: starting earlier and staying invested longer matters more than the exact return. Actual returns of market-linked investments vary and are never guaranteed, so treat the result as an illustration, not a promise or financial advice.

Tips

  • Try the same monthly amount over 10, 15 and 20 years to see how compounding grows.
  • Use a conservative return when planning for important goals.
  • Increase your SIP a little each year to reach your goal sooner.

Frequently asked questions

Are the returns guaranteed?

No. Market returns change. The result is only an estimate.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a simple calculator for planning ideas.

Are the SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Market-linked returns go up and down. The calculator only shows what would happen if the entered rate were earned every year.

What return rate should I enter?

Use a realistic, cautious figure. Check the long term history of the type of fund you are considering, and remember that past returns do not guarantee future ones.
