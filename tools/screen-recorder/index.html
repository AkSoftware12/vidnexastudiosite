Screen Recorder

The Screen Recorder records your screen, a window or a browser tab, with optional sound, and saves it as a video, all in your browser.

How to use the Screen Recorder

  1. Tick the microphone box if you want to add your voice.
  2. Press Start recording and choose what to share.
  3. Press Stop, preview the video and download it.

About the Screen Recorder

Press start, choose what to share and record tutorials, presentations or bug reports. Your browser asks for permission each time and the recording never leaves your device. Tab audio is available in some browsers, and a microphone can be added. Stop the recording and download the file. It works on desktop browsers; most mobile browsers do not support screen capture.

Tips

  • Close private tabs and notifications before sharing your screen.
  • Record a short test to check sound levels.
  • Use a desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge for the best support.

Frequently asked questions

Is the recording uploaded?

No. It is recorded and stored only on your device until you download it.

Can it record system sound?

In Chrome and Edge you can tick 'share tab audio' or 'share system audio' in the share dialog on supported systems.

Does it work on phones?

Most phone browsers do not allow screen recording from a web page. Use it on a computer.

Does it work on phones?

Most mobile browsers do not allow screen capture from a web page. Use your phone's built-in screen recorder instead.

Is the recording uploaded?

No. It is saved on your device only.
