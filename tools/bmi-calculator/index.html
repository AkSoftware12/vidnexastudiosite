BMI Calculator

The BMI Calculator estimates your body mass index from height and weight and shows which standard range it falls into, with a healthy weight range for your height.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Enter your height in centimetres.
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  3. Read your BMI and category.

About the BMI Calculator

BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared. Adults are generally grouped as underweight below 18.5, normal from 18.5 to 24.9, overweight from 25 to 29.9 and obese at 30 and above. Some health guidelines for South Asian adults use lower cut-offs, so ask a doctor which applies to you. BMI is a simple screening number and does not measure body fat, muscle or overall health. It is not medical advice.

Tips

  • Measure height without shoes and weight in light clothing for a better estimate.
  • Muscular people may have a high BMI without excess body fat.
  • Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making health decisions based on BMI.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It is a rough screening number and ignores muscle, age and body type.

Is this medical advice?

No. Please ask a doctor about your health.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It is a rough screening tool and ignores muscle, bone density, age and body shape.

Can I use it for children?

Not reliably. Children's BMI is judged against age and sex charts, so please ask a paediatrician.
