Video Info Viewer

The Video Info tool shows a video's resolution, duration, file size and estimated bitrate without uploading the file.

How to use the Video Info Viewer

  1. Choose a video file.
  2. Read the details shown instantly.
  3. Use them to decide how to compress or trim the file.

About the Video Info Viewer

Choose a video to see its width and height, length, size, type and approximate bitrate. This helps when a form asks for a specific resolution or when you want to know why a file is large. Information is read locally from the file and the video is not sent anywhere.

Tips

  • Compare the bitrate to see why one video is much bigger than another.
  • Check the resolution before uploading to a service with limits.
  • Use the Video Compressor if the size is too large.

Frequently asked questions

Does it show the frame rate or codec?

Browsers do not share those details with web pages, so this tool shows what they do share: size, duration, resolution and average bitrate.

Is the file uploaded?

No. It is only read inside your browser.

What is bitrate?

Bitrate is how much data is used per second of video. Higher bitrate usually means better quality and a larger file.

Does it show the codec?

It shows what your browser can read, mainly size, duration and resolution.
