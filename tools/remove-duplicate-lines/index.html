Remove Duplicate Lines

The Remove Duplicate Lines tool deletes repeated lines from a list and keeps each unique line once, in a single click.

How to use the Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your list, one item per line.
  2. Choose whether to ignore case, trim spaces and remove empty lines.
  3. Press Remove duplicates and copy the result.

About the Remove Duplicate Lines

Cleaning an email list, a keyword list, a set of URLs or log output often means getting rid of repeated lines. Paste your list, choose whether letter case and extra spaces should matter, and the tool returns only the unique lines in their original order. You can see how many duplicates were removed. All processing is local to your browser, so private lists stay private.

Tips

  • Turn on case-insensitive matching when 'Apple' and 'apple' should count as the same line.
  • Trim spaces first if copied data has invisible trailing spaces.
  • Sort the cleaned list afterwards with the Text Sorter if you need it in alphabetical order.

Frequently asked questions

Is the original order kept?

Yes. The first time a line appears it stays, and later copies are removed.

How many lines can it handle?

Tens of thousands of lines work fine on a normal phone or computer.

Does it keep the first or last copy of a line?

It keeps the first occurrence and removes the later repeats, so your original order is preserved.

How many lines can I paste?

Tens of thousands of lines work fine on most devices. The limit is your browser's memory, not the site.
