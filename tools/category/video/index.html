Free Video and Audio Tools

Free video and audio tools that run in your browser: compress, trim and speed up videos, turn a clip into a GIF, extract audio, save a frame as an image, check video information, record your screen and record your voice. Your videos are never uploaded.

Video CompressorMake a video file smaller, right in your browser.Video TrimmerCut the start and end off a video.Video Speed ChangerMake a video faster or slow motion.Video to GIF ConverterTurn a short video moment into an animated GIF.Video to Audio ExtractorPull the sound out of a video file.Video Frame ExtractorSave any frame of a video as a PNG or JPG image.Video Info ViewerSee a video's resolution, duration, size and bitrate.Screen RecorderRecord your screen, a window or a tab.Voice RecorderRecord your voice and download it.

Frequently asked questions

Are my videos uploaded?

No. Video and audio are processed on your device inside your browser.

Why does processing take as long as the video?

Browsers re-record video in real time, so a longer video takes longer. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
