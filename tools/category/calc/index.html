Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators accurate?

They use standard formulas and give close estimates. For official figures such as loan statements or tax, always confirm with your bank or an accountant.

Is my financial data saved?

No. Calculations happen in your browser and the numbers you enter are not stored or uploaded.
