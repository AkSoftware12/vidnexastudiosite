PDF Page Numbers and Watermark

The PDF Page Numbers and Watermark tool adds page numbers or a text watermark to every page of a PDF, with a live preview of the first page.

How to use the PDF Page Numbers and Watermark

  1. Choose a PDF.
  2. Choose Page numbers or Watermark and set the options.
  3. Check the preview of page 1.
  4. Press Download PDF.

About the PDF Page Numbers and Watermark

Add numbers in any corner or the centre, choose a format such as Page 1 of 10, set the first number and skip the cover page. Or add a watermark like CONFIDENTIAL or DRAFT across every page, with your own size, colour, opacity and angle. The preview shows page 1 before you download. Everything is done in your browser, so your document stays on your device. The watermark and numbers are added to the page, so they cannot be removed with this tool.

Tips

  • Skip the first page when your PDF has a cover page.
  • Use a light opacity of 15 to 25 percent for watermarks so text stays easy to read.
  • Keep a copy of the original, because the numbers and watermark cannot be undone.

Frequently asked questions

Can I skip the first page?

Yes. Tick 'Do not number the first page' and the numbering starts from the second page.

Does the watermark go on every page?

Yes. It is placed in the centre of every page.

Can I start numbering from a different number?

Yes. Change the Start at box, for example to 5 if the PDF continues another document.

Can I use a logo as a watermark?

Not yet. This tool adds text watermarks only. You can add a logo image with the PDF Editor.
