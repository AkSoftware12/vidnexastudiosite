Image Compressor

The Image Compressor reduces the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP photos right in your browser, without uploading them anywhere.

How to use the Image Compressor

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Set the quality and output format.
  3. Download the smaller files.

About the Image Compressor

Large images slow down websites and fail upload limits on forms and email. Choose your images, set the quality, and compare the original and compressed size before you download. JPG and WebP are compressed by lowering quality, which gives the biggest savings for photos. Because processing happens on your own device, your photos stay private and there is no waiting for uploads.

Tips

  • A quality of 70 to 80 percent usually looks the same but is much smaller.
  • Resize large photos first with the Image Resizer for even bigger savings.
  • Use WebP for websites; it is typically smaller than JPG at similar quality.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Compression runs inside your browser.

Which quality should I use?

Around 70 to 80 usually keeps photos looking good and cuts size a lot.

Is the quality reduced?

A little, depending on the quality you choose. At moderate settings the difference is rarely visible.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. The compression is done inside your browser, so the photos never leave your device.
