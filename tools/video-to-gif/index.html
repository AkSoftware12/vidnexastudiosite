Video to GIF Converter

The Video to GIF tool turns a short moment of a video into an animated GIF in your browser.

How to use the Video to GIF Converter

  1. Choose a video and set the start time.
  2. Pick the length, width and frames per second.
  3. Press Make GIF, check the preview and download it.

About the Video to GIF Converter

Choose a video, select the start time, length, size and frame rate, and download your GIF. GIFs get large quickly, so keep clips to a few seconds and reduce the width for sharing. A lower frame rate and smaller size produce smaller files. The GIF is built on your device and the video is not uploaded.

Tips

  • Keep GIFs under about 5 seconds for a reasonable file size.
  • Reduce the width to 480 pixels or less for chat and social sharing.
  • Use 10 to 12 frames per second to balance smoothness and size.

Frequently asked questions

How long can the GIF be?

Up to 10 seconds. GIF files grow very fast, so short clips at 320 px wide work best.

Why is my GIF large?

GIF is an old format. Lower the width, the frames per second or the length to shrink it.

Is it uploaded?

No. Frames are captured and the GIF is built inside your browser.

Why is my GIF so large?

GIF is an old format that stores every frame as an image. Shorten the clip, shrink the width or lower the frame rate.

Does the GIF have sound?

No. GIF files cannot contain audio.
