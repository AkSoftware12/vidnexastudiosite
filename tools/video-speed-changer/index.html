Video Speed Changer

The Video Speed Changer makes a video faster or plays it in slow motion and saves the result, in your browser.

How to use the Video Speed Changer

  1. Choose a video.
  2. Pick a speed from 0.5x to 2x.
  3. Press Change speed and download when it finishes.

About the Video Speed Changer

Choose a speed from 0.25x slow motion up to 4x fast forward and download the new video. The video is processed inside your browser, so the file is never uploaded, and processing runs in real time, so a longer video takes longer. It is great for time-lapse effects, tutorials and dramatic slow-motion clips. Audio speed changes along with the video.

Tips

  • Use 0.5x for gentle slow motion and 2x for a quick time-lapse.
  • Short clips work best for creative effects.
  • Keep the tab open until processing finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Does the sound change pitch?

Browsers try to keep the voice pitch natural, but very slow or fast speeds can sound odd. You can remove the sound if you prefer.

How long does it take?

It runs at the new speed, so a video sped up to 2x finishes in half the time.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your device.

Does the sound change too?

Yes. The audio plays faster or slower along with the video.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your device.
