Image Converter

The Image Converter converts pictures between PNG, JPG and WebP formats right in your browser.

How to use the Image Converter

  1. Choose the format you want.
  2. Add your images.
  3. Download each converted file.

About the Image Converter

Choose an image, pick the output format and download the new file. JPG is best for photos, PNG keeps sharp edges and transparency, and WebP is a modern format that is usually smaller on websites. When converting a transparent PNG to JPG, the transparent area becomes a solid background. Conversion runs on your device, so nothing is uploaded.

Tips

  • Choose PNG for logos and screenshots with text.
  • Choose JPG or WebP for photographs to keep the file small.
  • Remember that JPG does not support transparency.

Frequently asked questions

Why does PNG to JPG get a white background?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white.

Can I convert many at once?

Yes. Add several files.

Which format should I choose?

Use JPG for photos, PNG for graphics needing transparency, and WebP when you want small files for a website.

Does converting reduce quality?

Converting to JPG or WebP can lose a little quality because those formats compress. PNG keeps the pixels exactly.
