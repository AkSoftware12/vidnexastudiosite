Text Reverser

The Text Reverser reverses the letters, the order of words, or the order of lines in any text you paste.

How to use the Text Reverser

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Pick a reverse mode.
  3. Copy the result.

About the Text Reverser

Reverse a word to check a palindrome, flip a list upside down or create mirrored text for fun. Choose reverse letters, reverse words or reverse lines, and the result appears immediately. It handles ordinary Unicode text, and everything is processed in your browser without uploading anything.

Tips

  • Use reverse lines to flip the order of a numbered list or a log.
  • Compare the original and reversed text to test whether a phrase is a palindrome.
  • Emoji made of several parts may flip in unexpected ways, so check the result.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the modes?

Characters flips every letter. Words reverses word order. Each word flips letters inside each word. Lines reverses line order.

Does it work with emoji?

Most emoji work. A few joined emoji may split when reversed.

What is the difference between reversing letters and reversing words?

Reversing letters flips the whole text character by character, while reversing words keeps each word readable but changes their order.

Will it work on Hindi text?

It reverses the characters, but joined characters in Hindi may not look correct when flipped, so it is best suited to English text.
