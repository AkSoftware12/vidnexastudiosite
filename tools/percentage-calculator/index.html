Percentage Calculator

The Percentage Calculator finds a percentage of a number, what percent one number is of another, and the percentage increase or decrease between two values.

How to use the Percentage Calculator

  1. Pick the question that matches yours.
  2. Fill in the two numbers.
  3. The answer appears straight away.

About the Percentage Calculator

Use it for discounts, marks, tips, tax, growth rates and price changes. Choose the type of question, fill in the numbers and read the answer with a short explanation of the formula. For example, 20% of 450 is 90, and a rise from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is saved.

Tips

  • For a percentage change, always divide the difference by the original value, not the new one.
  • A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease does not bring you back to the start.
  • Use the Discount Calculator when you want the final price after an offer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

Can I use decimals?

Yes, decimals are fine.

How do I calculate X% of a number?

Multiply the number by X and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I find percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.
