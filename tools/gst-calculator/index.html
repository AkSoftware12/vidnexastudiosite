GST Calculator

The GST Calculator adds GST to a price or removes it from a GST-inclusive price, and splits the tax into CGST and SGST.

How to use the GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Choose the GST rate and whether GST is included.
  3. See the net, tax and total amounts.

About the GST Calculator

Enter the amount and the GST rate, such as 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%, and choose whether to add or remove GST. The tool returns the net price, the total GST and the CGST and SGST halves used for sales within the same state. It is useful for invoices, shop billing and checking a price quoted with tax. Rates change, so confirm the current rate for your product or service with the official GST notifications or your accountant.

Tips

  • Choose 'remove GST' when the price you have already includes tax.
  • For sales between states IGST applies instead of the CGST and SGST split.
  • Always confirm the correct rate for your item with an accountant or the official GST rate list.

Frequently asked questions

What are the GST rates?

The tool offers 3%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. Check the latest rate for your product with official sources.

What is the CGST and SGST split?

For sales inside one state the GST is divided equally between the centre and the state.

What is the difference between CGST, SGST and IGST?

CGST goes to the central government and SGST to the state when a sale is within one state. IGST is charged for sales between states.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by (1 + rate/100). For 18% GST, divide by 1.18.
