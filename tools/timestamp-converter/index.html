Unix Timestamp Converter

The Timestamp Converter converts a Unix timestamp to a readable date and a date back to a Unix timestamp, in your local time and UTC.

How to use the Unix Timestamp Converter

  1. Paste a timestamp, or pick a date.
  2. Read UTC, local and ISO formats.
  3. Copy what you need.

About the Unix Timestamp Converter

Unix time counts the seconds since 1 January 1970 UTC and is used in logs, databases and APIs. Paste a timestamp in seconds or milliseconds to see the date, or pick a date to get its timestamp. The current timestamp is also shown. The conversion is done in your browser using your device's time zone and UTC.

Tips

  • A 10-digit number is usually seconds and a 13-digit number is milliseconds.
  • Always check whether a value is shown in UTC or local time.
  • Store dates as UTC timestamps and convert them only for display.

Frequently asked questions

Seconds or milliseconds?

The tool detects both. Numbers below 100 billion are treated as seconds.

Which time zone is used?

Both UTC and your device's local time are shown.

What is a Unix timestamp?

It is the number of seconds that have passed since 1 January 1970 at 00:00 UTC.

Seconds or milliseconds?

Ten digits usually mean seconds and thirteen digits usually mean milliseconds.
