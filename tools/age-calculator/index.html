Age Calculator

The Age Calculator finds your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth, and shows how many days until your next birthday.

How to use the Age Calculator

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Optionally change the 'age on' date.
  3. Read your exact age.

About the Age Calculator

Enter a date of birth and, if you like, a different date to measure against. The calculator takes leap years and different month lengths into account and returns your age in years, months and days, along with total months, weeks and days lived. It is useful for forms, school admissions, insurance and eligibility checks. The calculation is done in your browser and the dates are not stored.

Tips

  • Change the 'age at' date to find out how old you will be on a future date, such as an exam or job deadline.
  • Check the exact rules of a form; some count age on 1 January or 1 July.
  • Use the Date Difference Calculator to count days between any two dates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I check age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the 'Age on date' field.

Is my date of birth stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser only.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap days and varying month lengths are included in the calculation.

Can I find the age on a past or future date?

Yes. Enter any date in the 'age at' field to see the age on that day.
