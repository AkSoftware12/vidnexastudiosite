Image to PDF

The Image to PDF tool combines one or more images into a single PDF file in your browser, with page size and order options.

How to use the Image to PDF

  1. Add your images and arrange the order.
  2. Choose page size: same as image, or A4.
  3. Press Create PDF.

About the Image to PDF

Select your JPG or PNG pictures, arrange them in the order you need and choose the page size. The tool builds one PDF that is ready to email, print or upload to a form. It is useful for scanned documents, photo notes and receipts. Your images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to any server.

Tips

  • Reorder the pages before creating the PDF so you do not need to redo it.
  • Use Fit to page so images are not cut off.
  • Compress large photos first to keep the PDF small for email or form uploads.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?

No. The PDF is built in your browser.

Is there a page limit?

No fixed limit. Very large photos need more device memory.

Can I put several images in one PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as you like and each becomes a page.

Is there a file size limit?

No fixed limit, but very large images use a lot of memory, so compress them first on older phones.
