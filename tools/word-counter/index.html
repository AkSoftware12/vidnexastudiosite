Word Counter

The Word Counter counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time as you type or paste.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Numbers update live as you type.
  3. Press Clear to start again.

About the Word Counter

Writers, students and content teams use a word counter to stay inside limits such as 280 characters for a post, 160 for a meta description or 500 words for an essay. This tool updates live, so you can trim a sentence and see the numbers change at once. Reading time is estimated from an average adult reading speed, which makes it handy for blog posts and speeches. Everything is counted inside your browser, so private drafts are never sent to a server.

Tips

  • Paste your text, then delete the sentences you can live without and watch the count drop.
  • Use the character count without spaces for SMS and form fields that ignore spaces.
  • Check reading time before you publish; most readers stay for posts under five minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count spaces?

It shows characters with spaces and without spaces, so you can use whichever your task needs.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything is counted inside your browser and nothing is sent to a server.

Is there a word limit?

No fixed limit. Very long texts of hundreds of thousands of words may slow an older phone, but normal documents are counted instantly.

Are my words stored anywhere?

No. The counting happens on your device and the text is not uploaded or saved.
