PDF Merge

The PDF Merge tool joins several PDF files into one document in your browser, in the order you choose.

How to use the PDF Merge

  1. Add two or more PDF files.
  2. Use the arrows to set the order.
  3. Press Merge PDFs.

About the PDF Merge

Combine invoices, scanned pages, reports or application documents into a single PDF. Add your files, drag them into the right order and press merge. Because the work is done on your device using a PDF library in your browser, your documents are not uploaded, which keeps sensitive files private. Password-protected PDFs may need to be unlocked first.

Tips

  • Check the order before merging, as it decides the page order in the final file.
  • Unlock password-protected files first, otherwise they may fail to merge.
  • Compress very large scans before merging to keep the result small.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDFs uploaded?

No. Merging happens on your device.

Why did merging fail?

A file may be damaged or password protected. Remove the password first.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The files are merged inside your browser and never leave your device.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Usually not. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then merge.
