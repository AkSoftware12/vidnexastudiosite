Case Converter

The Case Converter changes text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case and more in one click.

How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste your text.
  2. Pick the case you want.
  3. Copy the result.

About the Case Converter

Fixing text typed with Caps Lock on, formatting a headline or renaming variables for code are all quick jobs for a case converter. Paste your text, pick a style and copy the result. Title Case capitalises each word, Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence, and the developer styles join words without spaces. The conversion runs locally in your browser, so nothing you paste leaves your device.

Tips

  • Use Sentence case to repair text that was typed in all capitals.
  • Choose camelCase or snake_case when you are naming variables or file names.
  • Review Title Case results: small words such as 'of' and 'the' may need a manual fix to match your style guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which cases are supported?

UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case and aLtErNaTiNg.

Does it work with Hindi text?

Hindi has no upper or lower case, so those letters stay as they are. English letters in the same text are converted.

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word, while Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence and proper nouns you type yourself.

Does it work with Hindi or other languages?

Case only applies to scripts that have upper and lower case, such as English. Hindi and other scripts without case stay unchanged.
