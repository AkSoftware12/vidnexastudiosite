Video Frame Extractor

The Video Frame Extractor saves any frame of a video as a PNG or JPG image, right in your browser.

How to use the Video Frame Extractor

  1. Choose a video.
  2. Drag the slider or step frame by frame to the moment you want.
  3. Press Save this frame.

About the Video Frame Extractor

Load a video, scrub to the exact moment and save that frame as a picture. It is useful for thumbnails, screenshots and keeping a still from a clip. Frames are captured at the video's own resolution and nothing is uploaded.

Tips

  • Use the arrow buttons to step to the exact frame you want.
  • Choose PNG for the best quality and JPG for smaller files.
  • Pause on a sharp frame, as motion can blur some moments.

Frequently asked questions

What size is the image?

It matches the video's resolution, for example 1920 by 1080 for a full HD video.

PNG or JPG?

PNG keeps every detail. JPG is smaller and fine for sharing.

Is the video uploaded?

No. It stays on your device.

What resolution will the image have?

The same as the video, for example 1920 by 1080 for a Full HD clip.

Can I save many frames?

You can save one frame at a time as often as you like.
