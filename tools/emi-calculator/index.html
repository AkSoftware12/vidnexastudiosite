EMI Calculator

The EMI Calculator works out your monthly loan instalment, total interest and total payment from the loan amount, interest rate and tenure.

How to use the EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the yearly interest rate and the tenure.
  3. See your monthly EMI and the full breakup.

About the EMI Calculator

EMI stands for equated monthly instalment, the fixed amount you pay every month on a home, car or personal loan. Enter the principal, the annual interest rate and the tenure in months or years, and the calculator shows the EMI, the total interest paid and a year by year schedule of principal and balance. Try different tenures to see how a longer loan lowers the EMI but raises total interest. Results are estimates, and your bank's figure may differ because of fees and rounding.

Tips

  • Compare 15 and 20 year tenures to see how much extra interest a longer loan costs.
  • A small extra payment toward principal each year can shorten the loan significantly.
  • Remember to add processing fees and insurance when comparing offers.

Frequently asked questions

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

Will my bank show the same number?

It can differ slightly because of processing fees and rounding.

What formula does the EMI calculator use?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of months.

Why is my bank's EMI slightly different?

Banks may round differently, add fees or change the rate during the loan. Treat the result here as a close estimate.
