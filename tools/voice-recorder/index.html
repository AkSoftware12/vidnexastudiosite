Voice Recorder

The Voice Recorder records audio from your microphone and lets you play it back and download it, with no upload.

How to use the Voice Recorder

  1. Press Start recording and allow microphone access.
  2. Press Stop when you are done.
  3. Play it back and press Download.

About the Voice Recorder

Allow microphone access, press record and speak. Stop to listen and download the recording. It is handy for notes, interviews and quick voice messages. The audio stays on your device and is gone once you close the page unless you download it. Use headphones to avoid echo in your recording.

Tips

  • Record in a quiet room and keep the microphone close.
  • Download the recording before closing the page.
  • If nothing records, check your browser's microphone permission.

Frequently asked questions

Is my voice uploaded?

No. The recording stays on your device.

Which file format is saved?

WebM audio in Chrome, Edge and Firefox and MP4 audio in Safari.

How long can I record?

As long as your device has memory and battery. Keep the page open while recording.

Why does it ask for microphone permission?

Browsers require your permission before any site can use the microphone. You can revoke it at any time.

Where is my recording saved?

Only on your device when you download it. It is not uploaded.
