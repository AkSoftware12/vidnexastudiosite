Online Stopwatch

The Stopwatch is a simple online timer that counts up in hours, minutes, seconds and hundredths, with lap times.

How to use the Online Stopwatch

  1. Press Start.
  2. Press Lap to record a split.
  3. Press Pause or Reset when done.

About the Online Stopwatch

Press start to begin timing, record laps while it keeps running, and pause or reset whenever you like. It is useful for workouts, cooking, study sessions, presentations and games. The timer runs in your browser tab, so keep the tab open for the most accurate result, as some phones slow background tabs.

Tips

  • Keep the page open and the screen awake for long timings.
  • Use laps to record intervals without stopping the clock.
  • Write down your times, because they are not saved after you close the page.

Frequently asked questions

Does it keep running if I switch tabs?

Yes. It measures real clock time, so the total stays correct.

Is there a sound?

No sound is played.

Does the stopwatch keep running if I switch tabs?

It keeps counting from the real clock, but some browsers pause updates in background tabs. The time will catch up when you return.

Are lap times saved?

No. They exist only while the page is open.
