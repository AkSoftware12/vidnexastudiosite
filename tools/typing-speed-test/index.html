Typing Speed Test

The Typing Speed Test measures your typing speed in words per minute (WPM) and your accuracy in under a minute.

How to use the Typing Speed Test

  1. Read the passage shown.
  2. Start typing it in the box. The timer starts on your first key.
  3. See your speed and accuracy at the end.

About the Typing Speed Test

Type the shown passage as quickly and accurately as you can and see your WPM, accuracy and mistakes when the time ends. Typing speed is counted as one word for every five characters, which is the standard used by most typing tests. Practise a few minutes every day and your score will rise steadily. The test runs entirely in your browser and your results are not stored on our servers.

Tips

  • Focus on accuracy first; speed follows naturally once mistakes drop.
  • Sit upright and keep your wrists relaxed to avoid strain.
  • Try again on different days, as scores vary with practice and focus.

Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Every 5 correct characters count as one word, divided by the minutes you took.

Is a good score?

Around 40 WPM is typical. Above 60 WPM is quick.

What is a good typing speed?

Most people type 35 to 45 WPM. Above 60 WPM is considered fast for office work, and professionals often reach 70 or more.

How is WPM calculated?

Every five characters, including spaces, count as one word. WPM is that word count divided by the minutes taken.
