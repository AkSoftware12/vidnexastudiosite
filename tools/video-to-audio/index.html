Video to Audio Extractor

The Video to Audio tool extracts the sound from a video and saves it as an audio file in your browser.

How to use the Video to Audio Extractor

  1. Choose a video file.
  2. Pick WAV (fast, large) or WebM/Opus (small, takes as long as the video).
  3. Press Extract audio, listen to the preview and download.

About the Video to Audio Extractor

Choose a video and download its soundtrack as WAV audio, handy for saving a song, lecture or voice note from a clip you own. The video is processed inside your browser, so the file is never uploaded, and processing runs in real time, so a longer video takes longer. Only extract audio from videos you own or have permission to use.

Tips

  • Use it only on videos you have the rights to.
  • WAV files are large; convert them to MP3 in an audio app if you need small files.
  • Trim the video first if you only need part of the audio.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an MP3?

Not directly. Browsers cannot make MP3 files on their own, so you get WAV or WebM/Opus audio. Most players and editors open both.

Which option should I choose?

WAV is quick and works well for short clips. WebM/Opus makes a much smaller file but runs at normal speed.

Is my video uploaded?

No. The audio is extracted on your device.

What audio format do I get?

A WAV file, which keeps full quality but is larger than MP3.

Is the video uploaded?

No. The audio is extracted on your device.
