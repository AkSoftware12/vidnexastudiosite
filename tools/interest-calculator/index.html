Simple & Compound Interest Calculator

The Interest Calculator computes simple or compound interest and the final amount from the principal, rate and time.

How to use the Simple & Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter principal, rate and years.
  2. Choose simple interest or a compounding period.
  3. See interest earned and maturity amount.

About the Simple & Compound Interest Calculator

With simple interest the amount earned stays the same every year, calculated on the original principal. With compound interest you earn interest on previously earned interest, so savings grow faster. Choose the type, enter the principal, rate and period and, for compound interest, the compounding frequency. The result shows the interest earned and the maturity amount. Check your bank's own terms, as actual interest may be calculated differently.

Tips

  • Use compound interest to estimate fixed deposits and savings; use simple interest for many short term loans.
  • Choose quarterly compounding to match many bank deposits.
  • Compare the same amount over 5 and 10 years to see compounding at work.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference?

Simple interest is only on the principal. Compound interest is also on interest already earned.

Does it work for FD?

It gives a good estimate for a fixed deposit. Banks may round differently.

What is the formula for compound interest?

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is the principal, r the annual rate, n the compounding periods per year and t the years.

What is the formula for simple interest?

Simple interest = P × R × T ÷ 100, where P is the principal, R the yearly rate and T the time in years.
