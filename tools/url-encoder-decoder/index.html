URL Encoder & Decoder

The URL Encoder Decoder percent-encodes text so it is safe in a web address, or decodes percent-encoded text back to normal.

How to use the URL Encoder & Decoder

  1. Paste text or a URL.
  2. Press Encode or Decode.
  3. Copy the result.

About the URL Encoder & Decoder

Characters such as spaces, ampersands and non-English letters must be encoded in URLs, for example a space becomes %20. Paste your text or URL, choose encode or decode and copy the result. It is useful for query strings, links in emails and debugging redirects. The conversion runs in your browser.

Tips

  • Encode only the value parts of a query string, not the whole URL.
  • Decode a copied link to read what it really contains.
  • Check that plus signs and %20 are handled the way your server expects.

Frequently asked questions

Why encode a URL?

Spaces and symbols are not allowed in links, so they are replaced with % codes.

Which function does it use?

encodeURIComponent and decodeURIComponent.

What does %20 mean in a URL?

It is the encoded form of a space character.

When should I encode a URL?

Whenever a link contains spaces, symbols or non-English letters in its path or query string.
