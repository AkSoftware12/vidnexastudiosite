Text Sorter

The Text Sorter sorts lines A to Z, Z to A, by length, or shuffles them randomly, right in your browser.

How to use the Text Sorter

  1. Paste your lines.
  2. Choose a sort method.
  3. Copy the sorted list.

About the Text Sorter

Put names in alphabetical order, rank items by length or randomise a list for a draw. Paste one item per line, choose a sort mode and copy the result. Numbers can be sorted as text or by value depending on the option, and empty lines can be removed in the same step. Nothing is uploaded; sorting is done on your device.

Tips

  • Use the shuffle option to pick a random order for a giveaway or team list.
  • Sort by length to find the shortest and longest items quickly.
  • Combine with Remove Duplicate Lines to get a clean, ordered, unique list.

Frequently asked questions

Does it sort numbers correctly?

Yes. Numbers inside text are compared as numbers, so 2 comes before 10.

Can I shuffle a list?

Yes. Pick Shuffle to put the lines in random order.

Is the sorting case sensitive?

By default A to Z ignores letter case so 'apple' and 'Banana' sort naturally. Check the options on the tool for stricter modes.

Can I sort numbers?

Yes. Use the numeric option so that 10 comes after 9 instead of after 1.
