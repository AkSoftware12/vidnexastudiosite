Video Compressor

The Video Compressor makes a video file smaller by lowering its resolution and bitrate, right in your browser with no upload.

How to use the Video Compressor

  1. Choose a video from your device.
  2. Pick a size and quality, or remove the sound.
  3. Press Compress, wait for it to finish, then download.

About the Video Compressor

Choose a video, pick a target size or quality and download a lighter file that is easier to share on WhatsApp, email or a form with an upload limit. The video is processed inside your browser, so the file is never uploaded, and processing runs in real time, so a longer video takes longer. Keep the tab open until it finishes. The output format depends on what your browser can record, usually WebM or MP4.

Tips

  • Lower the resolution first, as it saves the most space.
  • Keep the browser tab open and in view until processing completes.
  • Try a short clip first to find the quality you like.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. The video is re-encoded inside your browser and never leaves your device.

Why does it take as long as the video?

The browser records the video at normal speed to create the smaller copy, so a 2 minute video takes about 2 minutes. Keep the tab open.

What format do I get?

Usually WebM in Chrome, Edge and Firefox and MP4 in Safari. Both play in most modern players and browsers.
