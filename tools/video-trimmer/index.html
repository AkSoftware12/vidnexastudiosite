Video Trimmer

The Video Trimmer cuts the start and end off a video and saves just the part you want, in your browser.

How to use the Video Trimmer

  1. Choose a video and play it to find your moments.
  2. Enter the start and end time, or use the current time buttons.
  3. Press Trim video and download the clip.

About the Video Trimmer

Pick a video, set the start and end points with the sliders or time boxes and preview the section before saving. The video is processed inside your browser, so the file is never uploaded, and processing runs in real time, so a longer video takes longer. Use it to remove awkward beginnings, shorten clips for status updates or keep only a favourite moment.

Tips

  • Preview the clip before saving to check your cut points.
  • Trim before compressing for the smallest final file.
  • Keep the tab open until the download appears.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video uploaded?

No. Trimming happens on your device.

Is it exact to the frame?

It is accurate to about a tenth of a second, which is fine for most clips.

Why is the clip WebM?

Browsers record in WebM (or MP4 on Safari). WebM plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, VLC and most phones.

Can I cut from the middle of a video?

The trimmer keeps one section, from your start point to your end point. Trim twice if you need to remove a middle part.

What format is the result?

It depends on your browser, usually WebM or MP4.
