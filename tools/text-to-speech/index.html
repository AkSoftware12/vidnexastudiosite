Text to Speech

The Text to Speech tool reads any text aloud using the voices built into your device and browser, with adjustable speed and pitch.

How to use the Text to Speech

  1. Type or paste text.
  2. Choose a voice and speed.
  3. Press Speak.

About the Text to Speech

Paste an email, a study note or an article and listen instead of reading. Pick a voice, set the speed and pitch and press play. The available voices and languages depend on your device and browser, so a phone, a laptop and a tablet may offer different options. Speech is generated locally by your browser's own speech engine and the text is not sent to our servers.

Tips

  • Slow the speed down slightly when you are learning pronunciation.
  • Break very long text into sections if the voice stops partway; some browsers limit one utterance.
  • Try a different browser if you do not see a voice for your language.

Frequently asked questions

Which voices are available?

The voices come from your own device and browser, so the list is different on each phone or computer.

Is any audio uploaded?

No. Speech is made on your device.

Why do I see different voices on different devices?

The tool uses the voices installed on your device and browser, which differ between Android, iOS, Windows and macOS.

Can I download the audio?

No. Browsers do not allow recording the built-in speech output directly, so the tool plays it aloud only.
