Slug Generator

The Slug Generator turns a title or phrase into a clean, lowercase, hyphen-separated URL slug that is safe for web addresses.

How to use the Slug Generator

  1. Type or paste your title.
  2. Choose options such as removing small words.
  3. Copy the slug.

About the Slug Generator

A good slug is short, readable and uses only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Paste a blog title and the tool removes punctuation and accents, collapses spaces into hyphens and gives you a slug you can paste into your CMS. Short slugs that contain the main keyword are easier to read in search results and easier to share. The conversion runs locally in your browser.

Tips

  • Remove filler words such as 'a', 'the' and 'and' to keep the slug short.
  • Do not change a slug after publishing unless you add a redirect from the old address.
  • Use hyphens, not underscores; search engines treat hyphens as word separators.

Frequently asked questions

What is a slug?

The readable last part of a web address, like my-first-post.

Are accents removed?

Yes. Letters like é become e so the link works everywhere.

Why use hyphens instead of spaces in a URL?

Spaces become %20 in a URL, which looks messy. Hyphens keep the address readable and are the common convention.

Does the slug affect SEO?

A short, descriptive slug helps users understand a link, and it is a small positive signal. Content quality matters far more.
