Parking Ustad

Parking Ustad is a car parking puzzle.

About Parking Ustad

Parking Ustad is a car parking puzzle. Tap a car and it drives out in the direction of its arrow, but only if the whole lane is clear; a car standing far away still blocks the road. Colliding costs a heart, and finishing a level with no collisions earns 3 stars. Hint, show-direction and remove boosters can help.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Parking Ustad?

Tap a car. It leaves in its arrow direction only if the whole lane ahead is clear, so plan the order.

How do I get 3 stars?

Clear the level without a single collision.

What do the boosters do?

Hint suggests a move, Show direction reveals car directions and Remove takes a car off the board.
