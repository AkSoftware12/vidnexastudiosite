About Teer Nikalo

Teer Nikalo is a free puzzle game with three puzzles in one: Arrows, Ants and Screws. In the arrow puzzle every arrow leaves in the direction its tip points, and it only gets out if the path to the board edge is clear; otherwise it collides and you lose a heart. Clear the board before time runs out.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play the arrow puzzle?

Tap an arrow. It exits in the direction of its tip if the path to the edge is free. If another arrow blocks it, there is a collision and you lose a heart.

What are the other puzzles?

Ant Sort moves ants between branches until each branch holds 4 ants of one colour, and in the Screw puzzle you unscrew only screws with no plate on top, sending each to its colour box or to the tray, which loses the level when full.

Is progress saved?

Yes, in your own browser. Clearing browser data resets it.
