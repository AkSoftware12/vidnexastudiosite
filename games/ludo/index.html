Ludo

Ludo is the classic Indian board game of dice and tokens.

How to play Ludo

  1. Pick 2 or 4 players and whether you play the computer or friends.
  2. Tap Roll. A 6 brings a token out of the base and gives an extra turn.
  3. Tap the token you want to move. Land on an opponent to send it home, unless it sits on a safe star or start square.
  4. You need the exact number to reach the centre. First player with all four tokens home wins.

About Ludo

Free Ludo for 2 or 4 players, against the computer or with friends on one screen. Roll a 6, capture tokens and bring all four home. Ludo is the classic Indian board game of dice and tokens. Each player has four tokens that start in a base, and you need a 6 to bring a token onto the track. Tokens travel clockwise around the board and then up their own coloured home column into the centre. Landing on an opponent sends it back to its base unless it is standing on a safe square, and the first player to bring all four tokens home wins. This version lets you play 2 or 4 players against the computer or pass the phone between friends. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a token out of the base?

Roll a 6. A 6 also gives you another turn, but three 6s in a row lose the turn.

Which squares are safe?

The four start squares and the four star squares. A token standing there cannot be captured.

Do I need the exact number to finish?

Yes. A token in the home column moves only if the roll fits exactly into the centre.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Choose With friends and pass the phone around. Each colour takes its turn.

Does the computer cheat?

No. It rolls the same random dice as you and simply picks a sensible move.
