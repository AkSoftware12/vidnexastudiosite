Bead Snake 3D

Bead Snake 3D is a fast runner game played on a 3D track.

How to play Bead Snake 3D

  1. Drag left or right on the track, or use the arrow keys, to steer the snake.
  2. Pick up white bead bundles to add beads. The number above the head is your length.
  3. Hitting a block costs as many beads as its number. Green blocks are cheap, red ones cost more than you have.
  4. Walls cannot be crossed. Run out of beads and the run ends. Each level is a little faster.

About Bead Snake 3D

Free 3D runner in your browser. Steer a snake of beads, collect bead bundles to grow and smash numbered blocks. Each block costs its number in beads. Bead Snake 3D is a fast runner game played on a 3D track. You control a snake made of beads that moves forward on its own, and you steer it left and right. Numbered blocks stand in the way: running into a block costs as many beads as the number on it, so you look for the cheapest gap. White bead bundles along the track add beads, and walls between the lanes stop you from sliding sideways. Each level is a little faster, and the run ends when your snake has no beads left. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I steer?

Drag your finger or mouse left and right anywhere on the track. On a keyboard use the arrow keys or A and D.

What do the block colours mean?

Colours compare the block to your length. Green blocks are cheap, yellow and orange cost a good part of your snake, and red blocks cost about as much as you have or more.

How do I get more beads?

Collect the white bead bundles. The number above a bundle is how many beads it adds.

Is it a real 3D game?

The track, blocks and beads are drawn in 3D perspective right in your browser, with no download and no plug-in.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
