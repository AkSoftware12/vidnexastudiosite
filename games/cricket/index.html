Cricket Batting

Cricket Batting is a quick timing game.

How to play Cricket Batting

  1. Tap Hit, the pitch or Space to bowl, then tap again when the ball reaches the bat.
  2. Perfect timing is a 6, slightly off is a 4, further off is 1 or 2 runs, and very early or late loses a wicket.
  3. You have 3 wickets. In Chase mode you must beat the target within 2 overs.

About Cricket Batting

Free cricket batting game. Tap as the ball reaches the bat: perfect timing is a six, a bad swing is out. Play 2 or 5 overs or chase a target. Cricket Batting is a quick timing game. The bowler runs in and the ball comes down the pitch, and your job is to tap exactly when it reaches the bat. Perfect timing sends the ball for six, slightly early or late gives a four or a couple of runs, and a very early or very late swing can get you bowled or caught. Choose a 2 over or 5 over innings to set a high score, or Chase mode to beat a random target within 2 overs and 3 wickets. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

When should I tap?

When the ball is in the yellow zone in front of the bat. Tap Hit, tap the pitch or press Space.

Why did I get out?

Swinging far too early or far too late loses a wicket. Letting the ball go through without a swing is bowled.

How many wickets do I have?

Three in every mode.

Does the bowling get harder?

Yes. Hard bowling is faster and the timing window is smaller. Easy is slower and more forgiving.
