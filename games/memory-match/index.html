Memory Match

Memory Match is a card-flipping game with levels.

How to play Memory Match

  1. Pick a level. Each level unlocks after you finish the one before.
  2. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair.
  3. Fewer moves earn more stars. Levels 10 to 12 give you only a limited number of moves.

About Memory Match

A card memory game with 12 levels that grow from 4 cards to 36. Earn up to 3 stars on each level. The last three levels have a move limit. Memory Match is a card-flipping game with levels. Flip two cards at a time and find every pair; fewer moves earn more stars. Each level unlocks after you finish the one before, and levels 10 to 12 give you only a limited number of moves. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Memory Match?

Pick a level, flip two cards at a time and find every matching pair.

How do I earn more stars?

Finish with fewer moves. Levels 10 to 12 also have a move limit.

Do I have to unlock levels?

Yes. Each level unlocks after you finish the previous one.
