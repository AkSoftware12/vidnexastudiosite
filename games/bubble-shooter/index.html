Bubble Shooter

Bubble Shooter is a classic aim-and-pop puzzle.

How to play Bubble Shooter

  1. Move your finger or mouse to aim, then release or click to shoot.
  2. Match 3 or more bubbles of one colour to pop them. Bubbles left hanging with no support fall and score extra.
  3. Every few shots a new row is added. If the bubbles reach the line at the bottom the game is over.

About Bubble Shooter

Free classic bubble shooter with three levels. Aim, pop groups of three or more, drop the hanging bubbles and clear the board. Bubble Shooter is a classic aim-and-pop puzzle. A shooter at the bottom fires coloured bubbles into a honeycomb of bubbles at the top. When the bubble you shoot touches three or more of the same colour, the whole group pops, and any bubbles left hanging without support fall down for extra points. Every few shots a new row is pushed in from the top, so you have to keep clearing. Pop the whole board to finish a level and start the next one with more colours. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I aim?

Move your finger or mouse to point the dotted line, then release or click to shoot. On a keyboard use the arrow keys and Space.

Can I bounce the bubble off the walls?

Yes. Aim at a side wall to reach bubbles hidden behind others.

What are the levels?

The board is refilled with a bigger mix of colours each time you clear it. Hard mode starts with more rows and colours.

When is the game over?

When the bubbles reach the dotted line near the bottom.
