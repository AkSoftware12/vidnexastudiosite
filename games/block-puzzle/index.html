Block Puzzle

Block Puzzle is a calm puzzle game played on an 8 by 8 board.

How to play Block Puzzle

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. It only drops where it fits.
  2. Fill a full row or column to clear it and score. Several lines at once and back-to-back clears score more.
  3. When all three pieces are placed you get three new ones. The game ends when no piece fits.

About Block Puzzle

A relaxing block puzzle on an 8×8 board. Drag the three pieces onto the board and fill whole rows or columns to clear them. There is no timer and no rotation, just planning. Block Puzzle is a calm puzzle game played on an 8 by 8 board. You get three pieces at a time and drag each one onto the board where it fits. Pieces cannot be rotated, so you have to plan. When a row or a column is completely filled it clears and you score. Clearing several lines at once, or clearing on consecutive moves, scores more. The game has no timer and ends only when none of your pieces fits anywhere. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate the pieces?

No. Each piece must be placed as shown, which is what makes the puzzle interesting.

How is the score calculated?

One point per block placed, plus a bonus for every line cleared. Multiple lines in one move and combos on consecutive moves add extra.

Is there a time limit?

No. Take as long as you like.

What is a good strategy?

Keep the board open, avoid leaving single holes, and save a straight line of space for the long five-block piece.
