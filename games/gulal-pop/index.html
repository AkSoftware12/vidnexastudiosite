About Gulal Pop

Gulal Pop is a colourful rangoli puzzle for the festival of colours. Tap a bowl and it rides the belt, blasting open the pixels of its own colour in the rangoli. Waiting spots are limited, and every finished rangoli is added to your collection.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Gulal Pop?

Tap a bowl. It travels around the belt and clears the open pixels of its colour from the rangoli.

When do I lose?

When the waiting spots are full and no bowl can be played.

What do I get for finishing?

Each completed rangoli is added to your collection.
