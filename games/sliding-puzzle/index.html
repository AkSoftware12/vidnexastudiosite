Sliding Puzzle

Sliding Puzzle is a classic tile puzzle.

How to play Sliding Puzzle

  1. Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it.
  2. Put the tiles in order. Hold Peek if you need to see the goal.
  3. Try to beat your best number of moves for each size.

About Sliding Puzzle

Frequently asked questions

How do I play the Sliding Puzzle?

Tap a tile in the same row or column as the empty gap to slide it, and arrange the tiles in order.

Can I see the goal?

Yes. Hold the Peek button to see the finished picture.

Is my best score saved?

Yes, your best number of moves for each size is saved in your browser.
