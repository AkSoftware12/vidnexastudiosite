2048

2048 is a sliding number puzzle.

How to play 2048

  1. Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide every tile.
  2. Two equal tiles merge into one. Plan ahead so the board does not fill up.
  3. Reach 2048, then keep going. Use Undo (or press Z) if you slip.

About 2048

A smooth 2048 with 3×3, 4×4 and 5×5 boards, animated tiles and three undos per game. 2048 is a sliding number puzzle. Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide every tile, and when two tiles with the same number touch they merge into one with double the value. Reach 2048, then keep going for a higher score. You can undo a slip with Undo or the Z key. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play 2048?

Slide tiles with the arrow keys or by swiping. Equal tiles merge when they collide.

Can I undo a move?

Yes. Use the Undo button or press Z.

Can I keep playing after reaching 2048?

Yes. You can continue and try for 4096 and beyond.
