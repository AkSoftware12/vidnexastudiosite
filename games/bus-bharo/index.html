About Bus Bharo

Bus Bharo is a queue-and-parking puzzle. Look at the passenger at the front of the line and send out a bus of the same colour. Parked buses take up limited spots, and the level fails if every spot is full and the front passenger matches none of the parked buses.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Bus Bharo?

Check the colour of the passenger at the front of the queue and send a bus of that colour.

When do I lose?

When all parking spots are full and the front passenger does not match any waiting bus.

Can I get extra space?

Yes. You can buy an extra spot with coins.
