Glow Chain

Glow Chain is a memory game of lights and sounds.

How to play Glow Chain

  1. Press Start and watch the lights.
  2. Repeat the pattern by tapping the pads, or press keys 1 to 4 (1 to 6 on Hard).
  3. One step is added every round. In Normal and Hard, one mistake ends the game. Sound starts only after you press Start.

About Glow Chain

A pattern memory game with Easy (3 lives), Normal and Hard (6 pads, faster). The pattern speeds up as you go. Glow Chain is a memory game of lights and sounds. Press Start, watch the pattern, then repeat it by tapping the pads or pressing keys 1 to 4 (1 to 6 on Hard). One step is added every round, and in Normal and Hard one mistake ends the game. Your best score is saved only in your own browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Glow Chain?

Press Start, watch the lights, then repeat the same pattern by tapping the pads or using the number keys.

When does the game end?

In Normal and Hard modes a single mistake ends the game.

When does sound start?

Sound starts only after you press Start.
