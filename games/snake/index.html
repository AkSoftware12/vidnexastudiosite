Snake

Snake is a classic arcade game where you steer a growing snake to eat apples without hitting walls, rocks or yourself.

How to play Snake

  1. Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on the board.
  2. Eat apples to grow. Golden stars give 3 points but disappear fast.
  3. Classic walls are deadly, Wrap-around lets you pass through, and Rocks adds new obstacles as you grow.

Frequently asked questions

How do I control the snake?

Use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard, or swipe on the board on a phone.

What are the game modes?

Classic has deadly walls, Wrap-around lets you pass through the edges and Rocks adds new obstacles as you grow.

What are the golden stars?

A golden star gives 3 points, but it disappears fast, so grab it only when the path is safe.
