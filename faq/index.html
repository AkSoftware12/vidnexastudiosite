Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Vidnexa Studio, our free tools, games and apps.

What is Vidnexa Studio?

Vidnexa Studio is an independent studio from India that makes Android apps, free online tools and browser games. Our apps are Vidnexa Player and Vidnexa: HD Video Downloader, with Papervo coming soon.

Are the tools and games on this site free?

Yes. All tools and games are free to use, with no sign-up or subscription. The site may show ads to cover running costs.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use every tool and game without an account.

Are my files uploaded when I use a tool?

No. Image, PDF, video and text tools run inside your browser, so your files and text stay on your device and are not sent to our servers.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. Everything is designed to work on phones, tablets and computers. A few features, such as screen recording, depend on browser support and are mainly available on desktop browsers.

What is Vidnexa Player?

Vidnexa Player is our Android video player app. You can get it free on Google Play from the Apps page.

What is Vidnexa HD Video Downloader?

Vidnexa: HD Video Downloader is an Android app for saving videos you own or have permission to download. It is available on Google Play.

Is there an iPhone version of the apps?

Our apps are currently available for Android on Google Play. The web tools and games work on iPhone browsers too.

How accurate are the calculators?

They use standard formulas and give close estimates, but they are not financial, tax or medical advice. Please confirm important figures with a professional or your bank.

How do I report a problem or suggest a tool?

Write to us using the Contact page. Please include the tool name and a short description of the problem.
