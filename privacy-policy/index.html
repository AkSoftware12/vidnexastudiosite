Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers the website vidnexastudio.com (“the site”). Privacy policies for our apps are on each app page: Vidnexa Player and HD Video Downloader.

Your files and text stay with you

The tools on this site work inside your browser. Text you type and files you choose (images, PDFs and so on) are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Information we and our partners may collect

  • Usage data such as pages visited, device type, browser and approximate location, collected through standard server logs and analytics.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used by our advertising partners.
  • Scores in games are stored only in your own browser (local storage). They can be cleared in your browser settings.
  • Emails you send us are used only to reply to you.

Advertising

The site may show ads from Google AdSense or other ad partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be handled by service providers that host the site, measure traffic or serve ads.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to info@vidnexastudio.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
