Vidnexa: HD Video Downloader Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how Vidnexa: HD Video Downloader (“the app”), published by Vidnexa Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information the app may collect

  • Device or other IDs such as an advertising ID.
  • Approximate location (not your precise location).
  • Crash logs and diagnostics to find and fix problems.
  • App interactions such as which features are used, to improve the app.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email address inside the app.

How the information is used

  • To keep the app working and to fix crashes.
  • To understand how the app is used so that we can improve it.
  • To show ads and measure how they perform, through our advertising partners.

Sharing

Device or other IDs and approximate location may be shared with third-party services such as advertising and analytics providers that help us run the app. These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Security

Data sent from the app is encrypted in transit. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your files and videos

Videos and files on your device stay on your device. The app uses the permissions you grant only to do what you ask of it.

Your choices

You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can also uninstall the app at any time. To ask about data, write to us at the email below.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
