About Vidnexa Studio

We make simple, useful things for phones and the web.

Vidnexa Studio is an independent studio from India. We build Android apps, free online tools and browser games.

What we make

  • Apps – Vidnexa Player and Vidnexa: HD Video Downloader are on Google Play. Papervo is coming soon.
  • Tools – 50 free tools for text, calculations, images, PDFs and developers. Most of them run entirely in your browser, so your files are not uploaded.
  • Games – quick browser games that need no download or sign-up.

How we think

Keep it fast. Keep it clear. Be honest about what a product does. We are a small team and we read every message.
