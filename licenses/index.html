Fonts in the PDF Editor

When you edit text in a PDF and the PDF does not contain the letters you typed, the editor uses a look-alike font. These look-alike fonts are reduced, renamed copies of Liberation Fonts, Carlito and Caladea (the names “VX Sans”, “VX Serif”, “VX Mono”, “VX Cal” and “VX Cam”), as the Open Font License requires for modified versions. They are not the original Arial, Times New Roman, Courier New, Calibri or Cambria fonts, and are not made by their owners.
