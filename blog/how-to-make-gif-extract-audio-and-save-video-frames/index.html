How to Make a GIF, Extract Audio and Save Frames From a Video

Three useful tricks for video files: turn a moment into a GIF, pull the sound out as audio and save any frame as an image, all free in your browser.

Video & Audio · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. Make a GIF from a video
  2. Extract the audio
  3. Save a frame as an image
  4. Tips

Sometimes you need only part of a video: a funny moment as a GIF, a song or lecture as audio, or one perfect frame as a picture. These tools do it in your browser without uploading the video.

Make a GIF from a video

  1. Open Video to GIF and choose your video.
  2. Pick the start time and a length of a few seconds.
  3. Choose the width and frame rate. 480 pixels wide and 10 to 12 frames per second is a good balance.
  4. Create the GIF and download it.

GIFs store every frame as an image, so they become large quickly. Short clips and smaller sizes keep the file easy to share. GIFs do not carry sound.

Extract the audio

Video to Audio pulls the sound track out of a video and saves it as a WAV file. WAV is uncompressed, so the file is large, but quality is full. Convert it to MP3 later if you need a smaller file. Only extract audio from videos you own or have the right to use.

Save a frame as an image

  1. Open the Video Frame Extractor and load the video.
  2. Move to the exact moment, using the step buttons for precision.
  3. Save the frame as PNG for best quality or JPG for a smaller file.

The picture has the same resolution as the video, so a Full HD video gives a 1920 by 1080 image.

Tips

  • Pause on a sharp moment, as motion can blur a frame.
  • Trim long videos first if you only need a section.
  • Keep the tab open until processing is finished.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my GIF file so big?

Shorten the clip, make it narrower or lower the frame rate.

Can I get MP3 audio?

The tool saves WAV. Use an audio app to convert it to MP3 if needed.
