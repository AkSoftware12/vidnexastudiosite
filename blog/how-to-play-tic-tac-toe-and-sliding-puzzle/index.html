How to Win at Tic Tac Toe and Solve the Sliding Puzzle

Winning strategy for Tic Tac Toe on 3 by 3 and bigger boards, and a layer-by-layer method for solving the sliding tile puzzle.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

Tic Tac Toe

Choose a board size and an opponent, then place your marks. On the 3 by 3 board you need 3 in a row, and on bigger boards you need 4 in a row. Hard mode on 3 by 3 never loses, so a draw is a good result against it.

  • Take the centre if you can, or a corner if the centre is taken.
  • Always check whether your opponent has two in a row and block the third.
  • Create a fork: a move that gives you two ways to win at once.
  • On bigger boards, build open lines with space on both ends.

Sliding Puzzle

Tap a tile in the same row or column as the gap to slide it. Put the tiles in order. Hold Peek to see the goal, and try to beat your best number of moves for each size.

  1. Solve the top row first and do not disturb it afterwards.
  2. Then solve the left column of what remains.
  3. Continue layer by layer, so the puzzle becomes smaller and smaller.
  4. Finish the last two by two area by rotating the tiles around the gap.

The puzzles are generated so that they can always be solved. Practice makes you faster.

Frequently asked questions

Can I always draw at Tic Tac Toe?

Yes, with perfect play from both sides the 3 by 3 game is a draw.

What does Peek do in Sliding Puzzle?

Hold it to see the finished picture while you play.
