How GST Works: How to Add or Remove GST From a Price

A simple guide to GST rates, CGST, SGST and IGST, with formulas to add GST to a price or remove it from a GST-inclusive amount.

Calculators · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. CGST, SGST and IGST
  2. How to add GST
  3. How to remove GST from an inclusive price
  4. Using the GST Calculator

Goods and Services Tax, or GST, is the indirect tax charged on most goods and services in India. Shops, freelancers and small businesses often need to add GST to a price or work out how much GST is hidden inside a price that already includes it.

CGST, SGST and IGST

  • When a sale is within one state, the GST is split into CGST (for the central government) and SGST (for the state). At 18% GST each half is 9%.
  • When a sale is between two states, IGST is charged as a single tax at the full rate.
  • The rate depends on the goods or service. Common slabs include 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, and rates are revised from time to time, so always confirm the current rate.

How to add GST

GST amount = price × rate ÷ 100. Final price = price + GST. For a ₹1,000 item at 18%, GST is ₹180 and the final price is ₹1,180.

How to remove GST from an inclusive price

Net price = inclusive price ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). For ₹1,180 at 18%, the net price is 1,180 ÷ 1.18 = ₹1,000, and the GST inside it is ₹180. A common mistake is to take 18% off ₹1,180, which gives the wrong answer.

Using the GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Choose the GST rate.
  3. Pick add GST or remove GST.
  4. Read the net price, the GST and the CGST and SGST split.

For invoices, the correct rate and place of supply matter, so confirm them with the official GST rate list or an accountant. This article is general information, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why is removing GST not the same as subtracting 18%?

Because GST was added on top of the net price. You must divide by 1.18, not subtract 18% of the total.

Which GST rate applies to my product?

It depends on the item or service. Check the official GST notifications or ask an accountant.
