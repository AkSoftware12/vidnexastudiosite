Memory and Reflex Games: How to Play Memory Match, Glow Chain, Mole Mallet and Sky Hop

Rules and tips for four quick brain and reflex games, plus an honest look at what playing games like these can and cannot do for you.

Games · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

  2. Glow Chain
  3. Mole Mallet

  5. Do such games help your brain?

Short games that test memory and reaction time are fun breaks, and each has a few simple tricks.

Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time and find every pair. Fewer moves earn more stars, and levels 10 to 12 have a limited number of moves. Tip: remember the position of every card you flip, even when it is not a match, and work in one area of the grid at a time.

Glow Chain

Press Start and watch the lights, then repeat the pattern by tapping the pads or by pressing keys 1 to 4 (1 to 6 on Hard). One step is added every round. In Normal and Hard, one mistake ends the game. Tip: group the sequence into chunks of three and say the colours quietly to yourself.

Mole Mallet

Pick a level and read its target score. Tap each mole as it pops up. Golden moles are worth 3 and you must never tap a bomb. Hits in a row build a combo up to x3. Tip: keep your eyes on the whole board, not one hole, and do not rush a tap on a bomb.

Sky Hop

Tap, click or press Space to hop through the gaps between pillars. Each gap is a point, and 10, 25, 40 and 60 points earn bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals. Tip: use short, steady taps and keep your rhythm.

Do such games help your brain?

Playing practises the specific game, and many people find it a relaxing way to focus. Research on whether casual games improve everyday memory or thinking is mixed, so enjoy them as entertainment and as a short mental break, not as a medical treatment. Regular sleep, exercise and learning new skills matter much more for brain health.

Frequently asked questions

Which of these games is best for memory practice?

Memory Match and Glow Chain both ask you to remember positions or sequences.

Are these games free?

Yes. They run in your browser with no download or sign-up.
