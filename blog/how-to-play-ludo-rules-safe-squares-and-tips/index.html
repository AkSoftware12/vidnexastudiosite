How to Play Ludo: Rules, Safe Squares and Winning Tips

The full rules of Ludo in plain words: getting out with a 6, safe squares, capturing, the home column and the exact finish, plus tips that win more games.

Games · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

Ludo is one of the most played board games in India. It is simple enough for children and still has real decisions in it. Here are the rules as used in our free Ludo game, followed by the tips that matter most.

The rules

  1. Each player has four tokens in a base. You need a 6 to bring a token onto the track. A 6 also gives you an extra roll.
  2. Tokens move clockwise around the board by the number rolled. If you roll three 6s in a row, you lose that turn.
  3. If your token lands on a square with an opponent's token, the opponent is sent back to its base and you roll again.
  4. Start squares and star squares are safe. A token standing on them cannot be captured.
  5. After one full lap, a token turns into its own coloured home column. It needs the exact number to reach the centre.
  6. The first player to bring all four tokens to the centre wins.

Tips to win more often

  • Bring more than one token out early. With only one token on the track you often waste good rolls.
  • Prefer moves that end on a safe square, especially when an opponent is one to six squares behind you.
  • Capturing is worth more than racing: it costs the opponent a whole lap.
  • Keep a token near your home column entry, so a late 6 is not wasted.
  • Count the squares. If the opponent sits exactly six squares ahead of you, you have a one in six chance to capture it this roll.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play Ludo offline?

Once the page has loaded, the game runs in your browser and does not need the internet.

Is the dice fair?

Yes. Every roll is a random number from 1 to 6 for every player, including the computer.

What if no token can move?

The turn passes to the next player automatically.
