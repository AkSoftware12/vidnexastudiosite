PNG vs JPG vs WebP: Which Image Format Should You Use?

Compare PNG, JPG and WebP for photos, logos, screenshots and websites, and learn how to convert between them for free.

Choosing the wrong image format can make a file too large or blur sharp text. Here is a quick guide to the three most common formats on the web.

JPG (JPEG)

Best for photographs. It uses lossy compression to make small files and works everywhere. It does not support transparency, and repeated saving lowers quality.

PNG

Best for logos, icons, screenshots and anything with sharp edges or text. It is lossless and supports transparent backgrounds. Photos saved as PNG can be very large.

WebP

A modern format that supports both lossy and lossless compression and transparency. WebP files are usually smaller than JPG or PNG at similar quality, which helps pages load faster. All current major browsers can display it, though some older software cannot open it.

Quick choice table

  • Photo for a website: WebP, or JPG if you need maximum compatibility.
  • Logo with transparent background: PNG or WebP.
  • Screenshot with text: PNG.
  • Photo for a government form or email: JPG.

Frequently asked questions

Is WebP better than JPG?

Usually smaller at similar quality, but JPG is more widely supported in older software.

Why did my transparent logo get a white background?

JPG cannot store transparency, so the empty area is filled with a solid colour. Keep the logo as PNG or WebP.
