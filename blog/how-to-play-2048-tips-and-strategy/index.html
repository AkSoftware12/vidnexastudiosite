How to Play 2048: Rules, Tips and a Winning Strategy

Learn the rules of 2048 and the strategies that help you reach the 2048 tile and beyond, including the corner method.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

2048 is a sliding puzzle played on a four by four grid of numbered tiles. It is easy to learn and surprisingly deep.

The rules

  1. Swipe or use the arrow keys to slide every tile in one direction.
  2. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile with double the value.
  3. After every move a new tile appears.
  4. Reach the 2048 tile to win. You can keep playing for a higher score.
  5. The game ends when the board is full and no merge is possible. Use Undo, or press Z, if you slip.

Strategy that works

  • Keep your biggest tile in one corner and never move it out. Choose a corner and mostly use only two directions, for example down and left.
  • Build a chain of tiles in descending order along one edge, so merges ripple along it.
  • Avoid pressing the direction that would move your corner tile away from the corner. If you must, plan to return it at once.
  • Keep the board tidy. Merge small tiles early rather than letting many different numbers fill the grid.
  • Think two moves ahead instead of making a move for a quick merge.

Why people enjoy it

The game rewards planning and patience. A session can last a minute or an hour, which makes it easy to play in short breaks. It is a good exercise in thinking ahead, and a session is just entertainment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the highest tile in 2048?

The game continues beyond 2048. In theory the largest possible tile on a 4 by 4 board is 131072.

Is 2048 luck or skill?

Both, but strategy matters a lot. Corner play greatly improves your results.
