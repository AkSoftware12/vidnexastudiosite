How to Rotate, Reorder and Delete PDF Pages, Add Page Numbers and a Watermark

Fix a messy PDF in your browser: rotate sideways pages, move or delete pages, add numbers and a watermark, and turn pages into images.

Images & PDF · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. Rotate, move and delete pages
  2. Add page numbers
  3. Add a watermark
  4. Turn PDF pages into images
  5. Good order of work

A scanned or merged PDF is often messy: pages sideways, a blank page in the middle, the last page in the wrong place and no page numbers. These four jobs can be done in the browser without uploading the file.

Rotate, move and delete pages

  1. Open the PDF Page Organizer and choose your file. Wait for the page thumbnails.
  2. Use the rotate buttons under a page to turn it left or right.
  3. Use the arrow buttons to move a page earlier or later.
  4. Use the cross button to remove a page.
  5. Press Download PDF.

You can also press Add another PDF to bring in pages from a second file, or Add blank page to insert a blank sheet.

Add page numbers

  1. Open the PDF Page Numbers and Watermark tool and choose Page numbers.
  2. Choose the position, such as bottom centre, and a format such as Page 1 of 10.
  3. Set the starting number. If your PDF has a cover page, tick the option to skip the first page.
  4. Check the preview and download.

Add a watermark

Choose Watermark, type a word such as DRAFT or CONFIDENTIAL and set the size, colour, opacity and angle. A light opacity of 15 to 25 percent keeps the page readable. The watermark is added to every page. Keep a copy of the original file, because the watermark cannot be removed afterwards.

Turn PDF pages into images

Use PDF to Image when you need a page as a picture, for example to put it on a slide or post it online. Choose PNG for sharp text or JPG for smaller files, choose the quality and optionally type the pages you want. Download single pages or all of them in a ZIP file.

Good order of work

  1. Merge your files first with PDF Merge if you have several.
  2. Fix the pages in the Page Organizer.
  3. Add page numbers last, so the numbers match the final order.
  4. Add a watermark if the file must be marked.

All four tools run on your device, so your documents are not uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo a watermark?

No. Keep your original file. The watermark becomes part of the page in the new PDF.

Why should I add page numbers last?

Because the numbers are written on the pages. If you move pages afterwards, the numbers will no longer be in order.
