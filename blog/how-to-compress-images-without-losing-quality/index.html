How to Compress Images Without Losing Quality (Free, in Your Browser)

Learn how image compression works, which quality setting to use, and how to make photos smaller for websites, forms and email without uploading them.

Images & PDF · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

  3. Get even smaller files

Large photos slow websites, fill storage and fail upload limits on forms. Compression makes the file smaller. Done well, you will not notice any difference on screen.

How compression works

JPG and WebP use lossy compression. They throw away fine detail that the human eye hardly notices, such as tiny colour differences. The lower the quality setting, the more detail is discarded and the smaller the file becomes. PNG uses lossless compression, which keeps every pixel but gives smaller savings on photos.

Steps to compress an image

  1. Open the Image Compressor and choose your photos.
  2. Set the quality. Around 70 to 80 percent is a good starting point.
  3. Compare the sizes shown for the original and compressed versions.
  4. Download the new files.

Get even smaller files

  • Resize first. A 4000 pixel wide photo is far larger than a web page needs. Use the Image Resizer to bring it down to 1200 to 1600 pixels.
  • Choose WebP for websites. It is usually smaller than JPG at similar quality.
  • Use JPG for photos and PNG for logos or screenshots with sharp text.

Why browser-based compression is private

The tool runs inside your browser. Your photos are never uploaded to a server, which matters for ID cards, documents and family pictures.

Does compression harm quality?

At moderate settings the loss is rarely visible. Very low settings create blocky patches and blurry edges, so check the preview before saving. Always keep your original file.

Frequently asked questions

What quality should I choose for a website?

Between 70 and 80 percent works for most photos. Check the preview if the picture has fine details.

Can I compress a PNG?

PNG is lossless, so savings are small. Converting a photo from PNG to JPG or WebP usually saves much more.
