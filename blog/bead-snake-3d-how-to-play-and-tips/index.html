Bead Snake 3D: How to Play and Tips to Go Further

How Bead Snake 3D works: steering, bead bundles, numbered blocks, walls and levels, plus simple tips to keep your snake long and reach higher levels.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

Bead Snake 3D is a runner game: your snake of beads moves forward on its own and you only steer it left and right. The trick is choosing which blocks to hit and which bead bundles to grab.

The rules

  • The number above the snake's head is how many beads you have.
  • Running into a block costs as many beads as the number on the block. The block breaks when its number reaches zero.
  • White bead bundles add beads. The number above a bundle tells you how many.
  • Walls between lanes cannot be crossed, so plan your lane early.
  • The run ends when you have no beads left. Every level is a little faster.

Tips

  • Look two rows ahead, not just at the next one. The cheapest block is often on the other side of the track.
  • In a full row of blocks there is always at least one cheap block. Find it before you arrive.
  • Grab bundles even if it means a small detour, as long as no wall blocks your way back.
  • Block colours compare the block to your length: go for green, avoid red.
  • Use Slow mode to learn the patterns, then try Fast for higher scores.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bead Snake 3D need a download?

No. It runs in your browser on phones and computers.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes. Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free, with no sign-up.
