How Browser-Based Tools Work (And Why Your Files Stay Private)

Understand how online tools can compress images, merge PDFs and edit video inside your browser without uploading files, and how to check it yourself.

Privacy · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. Your browser is a small computer program platform
  2. Examples
  3. Why it is better for privacy
  4. How to check it yourself
  5. Limits to know

Most online tools ask you to upload a file to a server. The tools on Vidnexa Studio work differently: they run inside your browser, on your own phone or computer. Here is how that works and why it matters.

Your browser is a small computer program platform

Modern browsers can run JavaScript, draw on a canvas, record audio and video, read files you choose and decode images. A tool page simply ships a small program to your browser. When you choose a file, the program reads it from your device, changes it and gives you the result to download. The file never has to travel over the internet.

Examples

  • The Image Compressor draws your photo on a canvas and saves it again at lower quality.
  • PDF Merge and PDF Split use a PDF library that runs in your browser.
  • Video tools replay the video and re-record it with the browser's recorder.
  • Text tools such as the Word Counter simply read the text box.

Why it is better for privacy

  • Your ID cards, bank statements and family photos are not sent to a third party.
  • There is no waiting for a slow upload or download.
  • Tools keep working even on a weak connection after the page has loaded.

How to check it yourself

Open the tool, switch your phone to flight mode or turn off Wi-Fi, then use it. A browser-based tool still works because it does not need the internet to process your file. You can also look at the network tab of your browser developer tools and see that no file upload happens.

Limits to know

  • Very large files depend on your device memory.
  • Video is processed in real time, so it takes as long as the video itself.
  • Features such as screen recording need browser support and your permission.

See the Privacy Policy for what the site itself collects, such as standard visit statistics and ads.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the tools offline?

After a tool page has loaded, most tools keep working without a connection, because processing happens on your device.

Do you keep a copy of my files?

No. Files you choose in a tool are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers.
