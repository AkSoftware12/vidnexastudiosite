How to Play Teer Nikalo, Parking Ustad, Bus Bharo and Gulal Pop

A guide to four Vidnexa puzzle games with rules, boosters and tips for planning your moves and clearing every level.

Games · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. Teer Nikalo
  2. Parking Ustad
  3. Bus Bharo
  4. Gulal Pop
  5. Tips for all four

These four puzzles reward thinking before tapping. Here is how each one works.

Teer Nikalo

Teer Nikalo has three puzzles in one.

  • Arrows: every arrow leaves in the direction its tip points. It gets out only if the path to the edge of the board is clear. If another arrow is in the way, there is a collision and you lose a heart. Clear the board before time runs out.
  • Ants: tap one branch, then another, to move the ants. Put four ants of the same colour on each branch.
  • Screws: only a screw with no plate on top can be opened. It goes to the box of its colour, or to the tray if there is no box. If the tray is full, you lose.

Parking Ustad

Tap a car and it drives out in the direction of its arrow, but only if the whole lane is clear. Even a car standing far away blocks the road. Finish a level without a single collision for 3 stars. Hint, Show direction and Remove boosters are there for hard levels.

Bus Bharo

Look at the passenger at the front of the queue and send out a bus of the same colour. Parking spots are limited. If all spots are full and the front passenger does not match any waiting bus, the level fails. You can buy one extra spot with coins.

Gulal Pop

Tap a bowl and it rides the belt, blasting open the pixels of its colour in the rangoli. Waiting spots are limited. Each finished rangoli is added to your collection.

Tips for all four

  1. Look at the whole board before your first tap.
  2. Start with the pieces that are already free and work towards the blocked ones.
  3. Do not use boosters on early levels. Save them for hard ones.
  4. If you get stuck, restart the level. A fresh plan often works better than undoing.

Progress and scores are saved in your own browser, so clearing your browser data resets them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download these games?

No. They run in your browser.

Are the boosters free?

Some boosters use coins you earn by playing. There is no payment needed.
