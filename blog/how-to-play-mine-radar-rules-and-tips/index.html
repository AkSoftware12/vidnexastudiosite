How to Play Mine Radar: Rules, Flags and Strategy for Beginners

Learn how Mine Radar works: reading the numbers, placing flags, the quick-open trick and simple logic to clear the board.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. The basics
  2. The quick-open trick
  3. Simple logic patterns
  4. Tips

Mine Radar is a logic puzzle: clear a grid of squares without digging up a hidden mine. No luck is needed once you learn to read the numbers.

The basics

  1. Tap a square to dig it.
  2. A number shows how many of the eight surrounding squares contain mines.
  3. A blank square means no mines are adjacent, and the game opens its neighbours for you.
  4. Mark squares you believe are mines with a flag, by pressing and holding or by using Flag mode.
  5. Clear every safe square to win.

The quick-open trick

When you have flagged exactly as many mines around a number as the number says, tap that number. All its other neighbours open at once. It saves many taps, but a wrong flag will make you hit a mine, so be careful.

Simple logic patterns

  • If a number equals the count of unopened squares next to it, all of them are mines.
  • If a number already has all its mines flagged, the other neighbours are safe.
  • Corners and edges have fewer neighbours, so start there when you can.

Tips

  • Start on the easiest level and learn the patterns.
  • Flag only mines you are sure about.
  • If you are stuck, look for a different area of the board instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I flag a square on a phone?

Press and hold the square, or turn on Flag mode.

Is the first tap safe?

In most versions the first tap is safe. Check the rules shown in the game.
