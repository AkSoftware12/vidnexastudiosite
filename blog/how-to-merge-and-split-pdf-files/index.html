How to Merge and Split PDF Files for Free Without Uploading Them

Step-by-step guide to joining several PDFs into one, extracting pages from a PDF and turning images into a PDF, all inside your browser.

Images & PDF · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. How to merge PDFs
  2. How to split a PDF or extract pages
  3. How to turn images into a PDF
  4. Tips for a smooth result
  5. Is it private?

Job applications, loan papers, college forms and invoices often need several documents in one PDF, or just a few pages from a long one. You can do both in your browser, without installing software and without sending the files to a server.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Open the PDF Merge tool.
  2. Add all the PDF files you want to join.
  3. Drag them into the order you need.
  4. Press merge and download the single combined PDF.

How to split a PDF or extract pages

  1. Open the PDF Split tool and choose your file.
  2. Type the pages you want, for example 1-3, 7, 10-12.
  3. Download the new PDF that contains only those pages.

How to turn images into a PDF

Use the Image to PDF tool. Select your photos or scans, arrange the order, choose the page size and create the PDF. This is handy for ID copies, bills and handwritten notes you photographed.

Tips for a smooth result

  • Check the page order before you merge.
  • Remove the password of protected PDFs first, as they may not merge.
  • Compress large photos before putting them in a PDF to keep it small for email and online forms.
  • Keep the original files until you have checked the final PDF.

Is it private?

Yes. The tools work with a PDF library running in your browser, so your documents stay on your device. This is useful for sensitive papers such as bank statements and IDs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge a PDF with images?

Convert the images to a PDF first using Image to PDF, then merge that PDF with the others.

Why will my PDF not merge?

It may be password protected or damaged. Remove the password in a PDF reader and try again.
