How to Play Snake: Modes, Controls and Tips for a High Score

Everything about the classic Snake game: controls, the Classic, Wrap-around and Rocks modes, golden stars and tips to beat your best score.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. Controls
  2. The three modes
  3. Scoring
  4. Tips

Snake is one of the oldest and most loved arcade games. You guide a snake around the board, eat apples to grow and avoid crashing.

Controls

  • On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD.
  • On a phone or tablet, swipe on the board in the direction you want to go.

The three modes

  • Classic: the walls are deadly, so hitting an edge ends the game.
  • Wrap-around: the snake can pass through one edge and come out on the opposite side.
  • Rocks: obstacles appear as you grow, so you must plan routes around them.

Scoring

Each apple adds to your score and makes the snake longer. A golden star is worth 3 points but disappears quickly, so take it only when the route is safe.

Tips

  1. Plan your path a few steps ahead, especially as the snake grows.
  2. Use the edges and make long loops so you keep open space in the middle.
  3. Do not chase every golden star. A safe route is worth more than 3 points.
  4. In Wrap-around mode, remember that you can turn at an edge instead of stopping.
  5. Stay calm. Panicky quick turns usually lead to crashing into your own tail.
