How to Calculate Percentage: 3 Easy Methods With Examples

Learn how to find a percentage of a number, what percent one number is of another, and percentage increase or decrease, with simple examples.

Calculators · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide

  2. 2. Find what percent one number is of another
  3. 3. Find percentage increase or decrease
  4. Common mistakes
  5. Use the Percentage Calculator

A percentage is a number out of 100. Percentages are used for marks, discounts, interest, tax, tips and growth. Three types of question cover almost every case.

1. Find X% of a number

Multiply the number by X and divide by 100. Example: 15% of 200 = 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

2. Find what percent one number is of another

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. Example: if you scored 42 out of 60, then 42 ÷ 60 × 100 = 70%.

3. Find percentage increase or decrease

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. Example: a price rising from 80 to 100 is (100 − 80) ÷ 80 × 100 = 25% increase. A drop from 100 to 80 is a 20% decrease, not 25%, because the base is different.

Common mistakes

  • Dividing by the new value instead of the old value when finding change.
  • Thinking a 50% rise followed by a 50% fall returns to the start. It does not: 100 becomes 150, then 75.
  • Adding two discounts together. 20% off then 10% off equals 28% off, not 30%.

Frequently asked questions

How do I turn a fraction into a percentage?

Divide the top by the bottom and multiply by 100. For example 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.

What is the difference between percentage and percentage points?

If a rate goes from 10% to 12%, it rises by 2 percentage points, which is a 20% increase in the rate.
