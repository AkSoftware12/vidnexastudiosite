How to Record Your Screen and Your Voice Online (No Software)

A simple guide to recording your screen, a window or a tab and your voice in the browser, with tips for clear sound and privacy.

Video & Audio · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. How to record your screen
  2. How to record your voice
  3. Tips for clearer recordings
  4. Privacy

Tutorials, bug reports, online classes and voice notes all start with a recording. Modern browsers can record your screen and microphone directly, with your permission, so you do not need extra software.

How to record your screen

  1. Open the Screen Recorder on a desktop browser such as Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press start and choose what to share: the whole screen, one window or one browser tab.
  3. Optionally allow audio, for example tab audio or your microphone.
  4. Press stop and download the video.

Most mobile browsers do not allow screen capture from a web page. On a phone, use the screen recorder built into your phone instead.

How to record your voice

  1. Open the Voice Recorder and allow microphone access.
  2. Press record and speak.
  3. Stop, listen back and download the recording.

Tips for clearer recordings

  • Record in a quiet room and keep the microphone close.
  • Use headphones so the speakers do not cause echo.
  • Make a 10 second test first to check levels.
  • Close private tabs and mute notifications before sharing a screen.

Privacy

The browser asks for your permission each time. The recording is made on your device and is not uploaded. It is lost when you close the page unless you download it, so save it before leaving.

Only record people, calls or screens when you have the right to do so.

Frequently asked questions

Why can I not record my screen on my phone browser?

Mobile browsers generally do not support screen capture for web pages. Use the phone's built-in recorder.

Is my recording stored online?

No. It stays on your device.
