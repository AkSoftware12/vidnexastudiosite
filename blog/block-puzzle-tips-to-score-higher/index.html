Block Puzzle: 7 Tips to Score Higher and Play Longer

Simple strategy for block puzzle games: keep the board open, plan for the long piece, clear two lines at once and avoid the traps that end a game early.

Games · 1 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

Block puzzle games look like luck, but most games end because of a few avoidable mistakes. These tips apply to our free Block Puzzle and to most block games on phones.

  1. Look at all three pieces before placing any of them. Sometimes the order matters.
  2. Keep one long straight gap open at all times. The five-block line is the piece that ends most games.
  3. Fill from the edges and corners inward, so the middle stays flexible.
  4. Never leave single-square holes. Only the one-block piece can fill them, and it does not come often.
  5. Set up double clears: a row and a column that will both complete with one piece. They score far more than two separate clears.
  6. Place the big 3 by 3 square as soon as it appears. It is the hardest piece to fit later.
  7. When the board is almost full, use the small pieces to open a line rather than to score.

How scoring works

You get one point for every block you place and a bonus for every line you clear. Clearing two or more lines in one move multiplies the bonus, and clearing on consecutive moves builds a combo. So a patient game that sets up big clears beats a fast one.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate pieces in Block Puzzle?

No. Pieces are placed as shown, which is why planning matters.

Is there a time limit?

No. The game only ends when no piece fits.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes, in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded.
