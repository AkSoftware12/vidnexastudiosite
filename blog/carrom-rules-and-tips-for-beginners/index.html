Carrom Rules and Tips for Beginners

How to play carrom: the striker, the coins and the queen, fouls, and a few tips for aiming and power that work on the board and in our online game.

Games · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

Carrom is India's tabletop game of flicks and angles. You hit a heavy striker with your finger to knock the small coins into the corner pockets. Our online Carrom follows the same ideas, so the tips below help in both.

The basics

  • There are 9 white coins, 9 black coins and one red queen. One player takes white, the other black.
  • Place the striker anywhere on your baseline and flick it. You keep your turn as long as you pocket one of your own coins.
  • Pocketing the striker is a foul: one of your pocketed coins returns to the centre.
  • The queen must be covered. After pocketing her, pocket one of your own coins in the same turn, otherwise she goes back to the centre.
  • The first player to pocket all nine coins wins. The queen adds 3 points.

Aiming

Imagine a straight line from the pocket through the coin you want to hit and a little further. The striker has to arrive at that point on the far side of the coin. In the online game, drag the striker to the spot on your baseline that lines up with that point, then pull back in the opposite direction and release.

Tips

  • Use less power than you think. A slow coin drops into the pocket; a fast one bounces out.
  • Use the cushions. A coin near a side wall can be pocketed by bouncing the striker off the wall first.
  • Break the centre pile with medium power so coins spread but do not fly into the opponent's pockets.
  • Leave the queen until a coin of yours is easy to pocket afterwards, otherwise you lose her again.
  • On Hard, the computer rarely misses an open shot, so do not leave your coins hanging near a pocket.

Frequently asked questions

How do I set the power?

The red line that appears when you pull back shows the power. A longer line is a harder shot.

Can I pocket the queen first?

You can, but she only counts if you pocket one of your coins in the same turn.

Does the game work on phones?

Yes. Drag with your finger. Landscape or portrait both work.
