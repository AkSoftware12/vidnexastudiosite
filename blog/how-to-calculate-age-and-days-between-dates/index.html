How to Calculate Age and the Days Between Two Dates

A clear guide to calculating exact age in years, months and days, and counting the number of days between two dates, including leap years.

Calculators · 2 min read · Updated 4 November 2026

In this guide
  1. How age is calculated
  2. Why leap years matter
  3. Counting days between dates
  4. Useful uses

Forms, exams, insurance and job applications often ask for your exact age on a certain date. Project plans and notice periods need the number of days between two dates. Doing it by hand is easy to get wrong because months have different lengths and leap years add an extra day.

How age is calculated

  1. Subtract the birth year from the target year.
  2. If the birthday has not yet come in the target year, subtract one year.
  3. Count the remaining months and days from the last birthday to the target date.

For example, from 15 August 2000 to 4 November 2026 is 26 years, 2 months and 20 days.

Why leap years matter

A leap year has 366 days because February has 29. A person born on 29 February has a birthday only every four years, and calculators usually treat the day after 28 February as their birthday in other years.

Counting days between dates

Computers convert both dates to a day count and subtract them. Decide whether to include the end date: from 1 to 10 of a month is 9 days apart, or 10 days if both the first and last day count. The Date Difference Calculator has an option for this.

Useful uses

  • Checking minimum or maximum age for an exam or job.
  • Counting the days left to a deadline, trip or event.
  • Working out a notice period or the days since a start date.

Calendar days include weekends and holidays. If you need working days only, subtract them separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does the age calculator work for future dates?

Yes. Enter any date in the age-at field to see how old someone will be on that date.

What if my form counts age on a fixed date?

Many forms use a cut-off date such as 1 January. Enter that date as the age-at date.
